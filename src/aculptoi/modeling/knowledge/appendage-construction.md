# Appendage construction

## Construction guidance

Place appendages only after their supporting body region is established. Give each major
appendage a clear base, direction, and silhouette contribution. For paired features,
build the intended mirror relationship before adding smaller detail.

## Evaluation signals

Check that appendages are visible, correctly attached, appropriately scaled, and do not
obscure the primary body. Verify paired features from both sides.

## Common failure modes

Floating pieces, roots hidden inside the body, an appendage too small to read, mismatched
paired features, and too many thin parts competing with the silhouette.
