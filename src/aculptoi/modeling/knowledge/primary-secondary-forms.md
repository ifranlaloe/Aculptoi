# Primary and secondary forms

## Construction guidance

Finish the dominant body and its broad secondary masses before adding fine detail.
Secondary forms should support the primary silhouette and be large enough to remain
readable from normal inspection views. Sequence work by visual dependency, not by the
ease of creating another object.

## Evaluation signals

Check that a clear hierarchy exists: one primary mass, a few supporting secondary forms,
then optional small details. Secondary forms should improve recognition without competing
with the body.

## Common failure modes

Many equally weighted parts, eyes or ornamentation added before proportions are correct,
and secondary elements so small or thin that they disappear in the atlas.
