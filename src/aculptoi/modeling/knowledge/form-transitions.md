# Form transitions

## Construction guidance

Make a feature emerge through a readable change in volume rather than a sudden detached
intersection. Overlap temporary masses when fusion is intended, then reshape or smooth
the attachment region so the connection supports the surrounding silhouette.

## Evaluation signals

Inspect attachment roots for detached gaps, abrupt pinches, implausible intersections,
and silhouette breaks. A feature should remain readable while its root belongs visually
to the primary form.

## Common failure modes

Appendages that look glued on, hard seams left after blockout, a narrow root that pinches
the body, and smoothing that erases the intended feature.
