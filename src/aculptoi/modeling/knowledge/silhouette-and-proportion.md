# Silhouette and proportion

## Construction guidance

Establish the widest, tallest, and longest relationships early. Make the outer contour
carry the primary identity before relying on surface detail. Check proportion changes from
orthogonal and angled views rather than optimizing one camera alone.

## Evaluation signals

Assess whether the object remains recognizable as a whole, whether its dominant masses
have intentional relative scale, and whether an important contour disappears from another
view.

## Common failure modes

A convincing single view with collapsed side proportions, overgrown secondary features,
an unreadable profile, or decorative detail used to distract from an incorrect overall
shape.
