# Bilateral symmetry

## Construction guidance

When a goal calls for left and right symmetry, establish mirrored placement and scale
before refining either side. Keep the central body aligned with the intended symmetry
plane, then add paired features with matching attachment positions.

## Evaluation signals

Compare left and right views for paired feature count, placement, scale, and angle.
Small intentional asymmetry should be distinguishable from accidental drift.

## Common failure modes

One missing counterpart, unequal fin or limb scale, mirrored parts attached at different
body heights, and perspective being mistaken for genuine asymmetry.
