# Elongated and serpentine forms

## Construction guidance

Use a few broad directional changes to establish a long form before refining its terminal
features. Maintain a clear flow from the primary mass through the length, with controlled
cross-section changes instead of repeated equal-sized segments.

## Evaluation signals

Check continuity of the long axis, progressive changes in thickness where intended, and
whether the form remains readable in profile as well as perspective.

## Common failure modes

Straight featureless tubes, abrupt kinks, evenly repeated blocks, disconnected terminal
pieces, and a profile silhouette that no longer explains the form's direction.
